## Product Overview
- **One-liner:** Everyday jewelry with a story: numbered pieces, released in chapters, made to stay.
- **What it is:** NOMAETstudios is a jewelry label (later also apparel) founded by Anahita Rehbein and Sascha Häcker. Each collection is a "chapter"; each piece is numbered. Chapter 01 tells their own story: two necklaces, two locks, one story.
- **Category / shelf:** Gold and silver everyday jewelry, small independent DTC label, Germany/EU.
- **Products (Chapter 01):**
  - **The “Lock” Chain** (DE: Die „Schloss“-Kette): 259–399 € (sterling silver / gold-plated / gold 585 on request; lengths 36/38, 50, 55, 60 cm). First edition; edition size **[open]**, never state a number until it is decided. "36/38 cm" = 36 cm chain with an extender to 38 cm.
  - **Pinky Promise Ring** (formerly "Key" Ring): 129–229 €, sizes 42–60, same three materials, small key engraved inside. Prices are placeholders **[open]**. Story: Anahita designed the ring herself and gave it to Sascha for his birthday, as a promise for good (EN line: "Anahita designed this ring herself and gave it to Sascha for his birthday, as a promise that lasts.").
  - Materials, finish, clasp, weights: **[open, waiting on jeweler]**.
- **Pricing model:** one-time purchase. No free shipping, no free returns. No welcome discount at launch.
- **Shipping:** DE 5.99 € (express 9.99 €), EU 13.99 €, international 19.99 €. Delivery DE 2–4, EU 4–8, intl 7–14 working days.
- **Launch:** shop goes live around January 2027, tied to the GmbH registration (exact date **[open]**). Instagram @nomaetstudios exists, no posts yet. No product photos yet.
- **Range:** jewelry is Chapter 01 only; further product categories (e.g. apparel) follow in later chapters. Never position the brand as a pure jewelry brand.
- **Channels:** shop nomaetstudios.com (Shopify), Instagram @nomaetstudios. About 80 % of buyers expected on mobile.

## Differentiation
- Positioning line: **"for the ones that were meant to be."** (DE: "Für Verbindungen, die kein Zufall sind."). Belief: fate, meeting at the right time, a bit of magic. Feeling: togetherness.
- A real origin: a promise the founders made to themselves, "no matter what" (met 2017, matching tattoos 2025, brand 2026). The brand stands on its own; the founders are behind it, not the face of every campaign.
- Chapters and numbering: every drop is a chapter with its own story; every piece numbered.
- Value from real materials (sterling silver, gold-plated, gold 585).
- Honest scarcity only. Never claim "handmade" until the jeweler confirms it in writing.
- Not like: Primark, Luamaya, Amazon (throwaway, interchangeable, anonymous). Aesthetic benchmark: Joseph Atelier (founder couple, drops).

## Brand Voice
- Warm, loving, approachable, real, free, soft, dreamy; never negative. Personal ("we"; when naming founders, Anahita first), calm, short sentences, full stops even in headlines.
- Concrete numbers over adjectives, but only numbers that are true.
- Brand language English; German for .de, service and legal.
- Signature lines: "meant to be." (stance) / "made to stay." (product) / "no matter what." (origin) (lowercase like the logo; the vow appears once per page; never used as a discount claim).
- Avoid: luxury, premium, must-have, exclusive, fake urgency.
